Balderdash

The game of balderdash & bluff

How to Play

Each round, an obscure word is revealed. Every player writes a convincing fake definition — then everyone votes for the one they believe is real.

Points:

  • +1 for guessing the real definition
  • +1 for each opponent fooled by your fake

Pass the device between players during the writing phase so definitions stay secret.
